FreezerFiles

Know what you have. Cook what you love.

FreezerFiles makes it easy to keep track of what’s in your freezer, see what has been there longest, and keep multiple freezers organized. Add food quickly, share a household, and spend less time digging through the freezer wondering what you already have.

How it works

Add food as it goes into the freezer and FreezerFiles keeps each batch organized by where it’s stored and when it was frozen. When it’s time to plan dinner, Use First helps surface food that has been there the longest.

Features

Know what’s in your freezer

See your current food at a glance without digging through a chest freezer.

Use older food first

FreezerFiles keeps batches organized by frozen date so it’s easy to see what you may want to use next.

Multiple freezers

Keep chest, garage, basement, kitchen, or other freezers organized separately.

Household sharing

Share a household through iCloud so the people you live with can keep the same freezer inventory up to date.

Quick Add

Frequently used foods and learned defaults make adding new freezer items fast.

History

See what has been used and quickly add familiar foods again.

Private iCloud sync

Keep your households and freezer inventory available across supported devices using iCloud.

FreezerFiles Plus

Unlock additional households, additional freezers, and household sharing with a one-time lifetime purchase. Basic inventory for your first household and freezer remains available without Plus.
